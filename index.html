How it works

Three steps.
No tools.

Your slab stays exactly as it is. The frame simply goes around it.

Step 1

Pick your card

Choose the design that matches your graded card. Every frame is made with the extended artwork of that exact card.

Step 2

Receive your frame

Black frame, direct UV print, milled precisely to PSA dimensions. Cut-outs for label and card – nothing gets covered.

Step 3

Slide the slab in

Slide the slab in from the top. Done. No glue, no tools – and it comes right back out if you ever sell.

Not graded yet?

No slab yet?
No problem.

Order your frame before your card even goes to PSA. Every frame ships with an empty display slab in exact PSA dimensions. Put your raw card in, display it today – and swap in the real slab the day it comes back.

Step 1

Empty display slab

Included with every frame. Same size as a PSA holder, clear acrylic, neutral OVERSLAB label.

Step 2

Your raw card goes in

Sleeve the card, slide it into the display slab, slab into the frame. Your card is on the wall tonight.

Step 3

Swap when graded

PSA slab back? Pull the display slab out, push the real one in. The frame stays exactly where it is.

The display slab is for display only – it is not a grading holder and carries no grade. It simply lets you use the frame before, during and after grading.

Details

Built around the slab.

Every frame is designed to the exact dimensions of the PSA holder. The label stays fully readable, the card fully visible – only the border becomes the stage.

  • Fits
    PSA standard holder
  • Inner size (slab)
    82.5 × 136.5 × 8 mm
  • Outer size (frame)
    approx. 118 × 182 mm
  • Material
    Acrylic, matte black
  • Print
    Direct UV print, lightfast
  • Insert
    Slides in from the top, no glue
  • Display
