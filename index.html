Why now

Overframe is new.
The frames for it weren't. Until now.

Since 2026, Konami has been printing cards whose artwork extends beyond the card frame. OVERSLAB is built for exactly that: the frame shows the part of the artwork the slab cuts off.

How it works

Three steps.
No tools.

Your slab stays exactly as it is. The frame simply goes around it.

Step 1

Pick your card

Choose the design that matches your graded card. Every frame is made with the extended artwork of that exact card.

Step 2

Receive your frame

Black frame, direct UV print, milled precisely to PSA dimensions. Cut-outs for label and card – nothing gets covered.

Step 3

Slide the slab in

Slide the slab in from the top. Done. No glue, no tools – and it comes right back out if you ever sell.

25 designs. Six series.

The most sought-after Overframe cards from every Yu-Gi-Oh! series – Duel Monsters to VRAINS. Hover over a frame.

Details

Built around the slab.

Every frame is designed to the exact dimensions of the PSA holder. The label stays fully readable, the card fully visible – only the border becomes the stage.

  • Fits
    PSA standard holder
  • Inner size (slab)
    82.5 × 136.5 × 8 mm
  • Outer size (frame)
    approx. 118 × 182 mm
  • Material
    Acrylic, matte black
  • Print
    Direct UV print, lightfast
  • Insert
    Slides in from the top, no glue
  • Display
    Stand & wall mount
